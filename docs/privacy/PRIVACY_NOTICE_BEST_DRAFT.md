## What we collect and why

| When you… | We collect | Why | Legal basis (Thai PDPA) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Send a private-experience inquiry | Name, WhatsApp number, email (optional), the experience you chose, occasion, preferred date, group size, budget, preferences and any details you add | To reply and prepare a proposal | Steps you asked us to take before a contract (s.24(3)) |
| Use the contact form | Name, WhatsApp number, message | To reply | Steps before a contract / legitimate interest in answering you (s.24(3), s.24(5)) |
| Join an event waitlist (e.g. New in Bangkok) | Email, first name (optional), which event, the time you signed up and the exact wording you agreed to | To email you when new dates for **that event** open — nothing else | Your consent (s.19). Withdraw any time via the unsubscribe link |
| Book a Single Meet Club place | Your name, email, WhatsApp, the names of guests you book for, confirmation that everyone is 20+, acceptance of booking terms | To issue tickets, manage the guest list and send event information | Contract (s.24(3)); legal obligations for payment records (s.24(6)) |
| Tick "tell me about future Single Meet Club nights" | Your choice and contact details | To tell you about future SMC nights by email or WhatsApp | Your consent (s.19) |
| Pay online | Payment is handled by Stripe. We receive a payment reference and status, not your full card details | Take payment, refunds | Contract; legal obligation |
| Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram | What you send | To reply | As above for the request concerned |
| Browse the site | Device and usage data via cookies and the Meta Pixel (see Cookies) | Advertising measurement | [CONFIRM: consent, once the cookie banner is in place] |

We don't ask for sensitive data (health, religion, etc.). Please don't include it in free-text fields. We don't sell personal data.

## Event and marketing communications

- **Waitlist emails** only announce new dates for the event you signed up for. Each email has an unsubscribe link. Signing up for one event is not consent to hear about other events, other brands or general offers; a new event or brand would ask you separately.
- **Booking messages** (confirmations, meeting point, changes) are part of the service and are sent whether or not you opted in to marketing.
- **Marketing** is only sent if you opted in (e.g. the optional Single Meet Club checkbox). Opt out any time by replying, via the unsubscribe link, or on WhatsApp.

Only service providers that help us run the service, under their terms:

- **Supabase** — database hosting (Singapore)
- **Vercel** — website hosting (servers may be in the USA and elsewhere)
- **Resend** — sending our emails
- **Stripe** — payments
- **Google** — our team inbox (Gmail) where inquiry notifications arrive; web fonts
- **Meta** — WhatsApp/Instagram if you contact us there; Meta Pixel (see Cookies)
- **Partner venues** — for bookings and private experiences, we share what a venue needs to host you (usually name and group size) [CONFIRM: is this accurate for your venues?]

We may disclose data if required by Thai law or a competent authority.

## How long we keep data

[CONFIRM — recommended defaults]
- Inquiries and contact messages that don't lead to a booking: **24 months** from the last contact, then deleted.
- Bookings and payment records: **as long as Thai accounting and tax law requires** [CONFIRM: commonly 5–7 years], then deleted.
- Waitlist: until you unsubscribe or **24 months** after you joined without any announcement, whichever is first. A record that you unsubscribed is kept so we don't email you again.
- Notification emails in our inbox: deleted on the same schedule as the inquiry.

## Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to access or get a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect earlier processing), and receive it in a portable format where applicable. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

To make a request, contact us at **[CONFIRM: privacy contact]**. We may need to confirm your identity. We'll respond within 30 days.

Some booking/payment records must be kept for legal reasons even if you ask us to delete them; we'll tell you if that applies.
