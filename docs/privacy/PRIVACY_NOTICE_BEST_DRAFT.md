## 3. Event announcements and marketing

- **Waitlist emails** only announce new dates for the event you signed up for. Each email contains an unsubscribe link. Joining one waitlist is not permission to contact you about other events, other brands or general offers. If we launch a new or materially different event, we will ask separately.
- **Booking messages** (confirmations, tickets, meeting-point details, changes or cancellations) are part of the service and are sent whether or not you opted in to marketing.
- **Marketing** is only sent if you opted in (for example, the optional Single Meet Club box). You can opt out at any time by using the unsubscribe link, replying to the email, or messaging us on WhatsApp.

To make a request, email **bookings@bestnightlifethailand.com**. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days [LEGAL: confirm response period]. Some booking and payment records must be kept for legal reasons even if you ask us to delete them; if so, we will tell you.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time with **"Cookie settings"** at the bottom of our pages.

- **Necessary:** keep the site working and remember your cookie choice (cookie `snx_consent`, 12 months). Always on.
- **Advertising — Meta Pixel (only if you choose "Accept"):** helps us measure our Facebook and Instagram ads and reach people who visited our site. Meta sets cookies such as `_fbp` (and `_fbc` if you arrived from an ad) and receives information such as your browser, the pages you visit, and events like a completed inquiry or booking. If you choose "Only necessary" — or don't choose — the Meta Pixel does not load. If you withdraw consent, we stop loading it and remove these cookies from our site.
- **Other providers:** web fonts are loaded from Google; payments take place on Stripe's pages under Stripe's policies.

The Meta Pixel never runs on staff pages, ticket pages or unsubscribe pages.
