## 7. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019), subject to its conditions, you may access and obtain a copy of your data, have it corrected, deleted or anonymised, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format where applicable, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

Requests: **bookings@bestnightlifethailand.com**. We may need to confirm your identity and aim to respond within 30 days [LEGAL]. Booking and payment records may have to be kept for legal reasons; we will tell you if so. If you booked through a platform, you can also contact that platform about the data it holds.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

On your first visit we ask whether you accept non-essential cookies. Change your choice any time with **"Cookie settings"** at the bottom of our pages.

- **Necessary:** keep the site and checkout working and remember your choice (cookie `snx_consent`, 12 months). Stripe's checkout uses its own necessary cookies.
- **Advertising — Meta Pixel (only if you accept):** measures our ads and helps us reach people who visited; Meta sets cookies such as `_fbp`/`_fbc`. Not loaded if you choose "Only necessary" or make no choice; removed from our site if you withdraw.
- **Guest reviews (Elfsight) and map (Google Maps):** these embedded services may set their own cookies, so they load only if you accept, or if you click "Show reviews" / "Show map" for that page.
- **Web fonts** are loaded from Google.

The Meta Pixel never runs on ticket pages or staff pages.

## 9. Security

Access to booking data is limited to authorised staff; connections are encrypted; we use providers with recognised security practices. If a breach affects you, we will act as the PDPA requires. [LEGAL]
